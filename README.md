# PSET PANIC by Jiaxuan Lin

A Canvas boss battle: fight your way to submitting your 6.1000 problem set that is due in 12 minutes...

▶ Play it in browser: https://jiaxuan-lin.github.io/pset-panic/

It's 11:47 PM and Problem Set 5 is due at 11:59. The page looks like a normal Canvas dashboard, but four bosses guard every step between you and the Turn In button. Your cursor is your character, so dodge their various attacks, fight back, and submit that PSET anyway.

## How to play

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| 🖱️ Move | Your cursor is your character. Dodge the attacks. |
| 👊 Click the boss | Punch it and knock it back, but don't get hit. |
| ⚡ Energy orbs | Move over the yellow orbs to power up your punches and heal. (Red ⚠ orbs are traps!) |
| 🎯 Goal | Once the boss is down, click the glowing button to move to the next step of submitting your PSET. |
| 🔇 M key | Mute or unmute the game's sound. |

## The bosses

Each step of submitting has its own boss, and each one is faster and tougher than the last.

| Step | Goal | Boss |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the 6.1000 class | 😈 Syllabus Imp |
| 2 | Open Problem Set 5 | 👹 Deadline Demon |
| 3 | Click Submit Assignment | 🤖 Autograder Golem |
| 4 | Click Turn In | 👽 Professor Overlord |

Bosses move between you and your goal, chase your cursor, and grab energy orbs before you can. Their attacks include aimed lasers, slams that track your cursor, walls that close in, screen-wide dashes that sweep your cursor away, and fake buttons that hurt you if you click them. After you press Turn In, you still have to survive the upload...

## Built with

One self-contained `index.html` file: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with a `<canvas>` overlay. It uses no libraries, images or build step, and all sound effects are generated in the browser with the Web Audio API. To run it locally, download the file and open it in any modern browser. A mouse or trackpad works best.

Created when experimenting with Factory's Droid at the MIT Women in EECS event on Oct 15 2026. 
Done with just one prompt and difficulty scaling afterwards. Presented.
